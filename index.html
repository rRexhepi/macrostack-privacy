Privacy Policy  ·  Apple / iOS  ·  Last updated April 2026

MacroStack AI ("the App") is a nutrition tracking app for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and how your data is protected. We take privacy seriously and collect only what is strictly necessary to provide the app's features.

Looking for the Android privacy policy? View it here.

1. Data Storage On-device only

Your nutrition logs, food entries, weight records, profile information, and goals are stored locally on your device. This data is never transmitted to our servers or any third party. We do not maintain any databases that store your personal health or nutrition data.

2. Camera & Photo Library Access

The App uses your camera to scan food and barcodes for nutrition tracking. You may also select photos from your photo library for AI-powered food analysis. When you use the AI Food Scanner, your food photo is sent to a cloud AI service (Google Gemini) for analysis. Photos are processed in real time and are not stored on any server after analysis is complete. No photos are ever shared with third parties beyond this processing step.

3. Location Access

The App requests location access solely to help you find nearby restaurants in the Coach feature. Your location is sent to mapping services to perform the search. No health, nutrition, or fitness data is ever combined with or sent alongside your location. You can deny or revoke location access at any time in your device settings.

4. AI Features

The App uses cloud-based AI (Google Gemini) to power the nutrition coach and food scanner. When you use these features, your messages and food photos are sent to our server, which forwards them to Google's AI service for processing. The data sent includes:

  • Your coach messages and questions
  • Your daily calorie and macro context (goal, consumed, remaining)
  • Food photos (for the AI scanner only)

No personal identifiers (name, email, account info) are included in AI requests. A randomly generated device identifier is used solely for rate limiting purposes. Google's use of this data is governed by the Gemini API Terms of Service.

5. Food Database

The App includes a bundled food database stored on your device for nutrition lookups. For barcode scans and certain searches, the App may also query an external food database over the internet to retrieve additional nutrition information. In those cases, only the food name or barcode is sent. No personal data, account information, or device identifiers are included in these requests.

6. Rate Limiting

To ensure fair usage, the App generates a random device identifier (UUID) on first launch. This identifier is used only to enforce daily usage limits on AI features. It is not linked to your identity, Apple ID, or any personal information. The identifier is stored on your device and on our rate-limiting server. No usage data, message content, or personal information is stored alongside it.

7. Data Export

The App allows you to export your data as a backup file, with optional password-based encryption. Exported files are created temporarily on your device and shared only through the method you choose (e.g., AirDrop, Files). We never receive or have access to your exported data.

8. Analytics & Crash Reporting

The App does not collect analytics, advertising data, or crash reports. No usage data is transmitted.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
