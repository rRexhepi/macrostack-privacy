Privacy Policy  ·  Last updated March 2026

MacroStack AI ("the App") is a nutrition tracking app built for iOS. This policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and how your data is protected. We take privacy seriously and collect only what is strictly necessary to provide the app's features.

1. Data Stored on Your Device On-device only

All food logs, macro entries, recipes, and nutrition history are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use an iCloud backup.

  • Food entries (name, calories, macros, serving size, date)
  • Custom recipes and meal logs
  • Daily nutrition goals you set
  • App preferences and settings

We do not operate any servers, databases, or cloud infrastructure that stores your personal data.

2. Camera & Depth Sensor

The App requests access to your device's camera and, on supported devices, the LiDAR/depth sensor for the following purposes:

  • Barcode scanning — to look up packaged food nutrition data from a local database
  • AI food recognition — to identify food items and estimate portion sizes using on-device or cloud AI (see Section 3)
  • Depth sensing — to estimate food volume and portion size on supported devices

Camera images and depth data are processed in real time and are never stored, uploaded, or shared.

3. AI Features & Third-Party Services

The App uses AI to identify food and estimate nutrition. Depending on your device and configuration:

  • On-device AI (Gemini Nano via ML Kit) — runs entirely on your device. No data is sent externally. Available on supported Pixel and other devices.
  • Cloud AI (Google Gemini API) — if you choose to enter a Gemini API key, food descriptions (text only — no images) are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate nutrition estimates. This is optional and only active if you provide a key. Data sent is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Your Gemini API key is stored locally in your device's SharedPreferences/Keychain and is never transmitted to us.

4. Food Database

The App includes a local copy of the Open Food Facts database for barcode lookups. All lookups happen entirely on-device. No queries or identifiers are sent to any external server.

5. Analytics & Crash Reporting

The App does not include any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or crash reporting services. No usage data or crash logs are collected or transmitted.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
